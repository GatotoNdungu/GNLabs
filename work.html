Hardware / IoT

IoT multi-sensor fire alarm

Built on an ESP32, fusing gas (MQ-2), temperature/humidity (DHT11), and flame (KY-026) readings through both threshold logic and a weighted-scoring model, so no single sensor failure trips a false negative. Notification routes across Wi-Fi, GSM SMS, and Twilio depending on what's actually available at the time, with GPS tagging on every alert and a local LCD readout for anyone standing in front of the unit.

Group research project, supervised
