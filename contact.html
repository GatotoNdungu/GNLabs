    Before you write

    Common questions

    A little preparation makes the first reply far more useful.

    What should I include in the message?

    What the system is, what it's doing that it shouldn't (or what you want it to do that it can't yet), and anything you've already tried. Photos, model numbers and error messages help a great deal.

    How quickly will I hear back?

    Within one business day. If your matter is urgent, WhatsApp or a call is the fastest route.

    What if you're not the right fit?

    We'll say so plainly and, where we can, point you to someone better placed. A quick honest answer is more useful than a long proposal for work we shouldn't take.

    Is there a cost to the first conversation?

    No. The first conversation is about understanding the problem. Anything chargeable is quoted in writing before work begins.

    Is my information kept private?

    Yes. Details about your systems are used only to respond to you and are never shared. We're happy to sign an NDA before you send anything sensitive.
