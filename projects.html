Batch Mixing Line Retrofit

A four-tank mixing line was still running on relay logic commissioned in the nineties. Batch consistency drifted with ambient temperature, and the only diagnostic was an operator's ear. The brief was to modernise control without taking the line down for more than a single weekend.

Approach

Modelled each tank's thermal response in simulation, tuned PID loops against that model, then validated on a hardware-in-the-loop bench before touching the plant. Cutover was staged tank by tank.

Constraint

52-hour commissioning window, with full rollback to relay control available at every stage.

How an engagement runs

The same four stages on every project, whether it's a two-week diagnostic or a six-month retrofit.

01 / SCOPE

Walk the system

On-site or over a call, we map what exists now — including the undocumented workarounds every plant accumulates.

02 / MODEL

Prove it off-line

Control logic is simulated and bench-tested against a model before it's allowed near live equipment.

03 / COMMISSION

Stage the cutover

Deployment happens in reversible stages, with a tested rollback path available at every step.

04 / HAND OVER

Document for a stranger

You get drawings, commented source, and a runbook written for an engineer who's never met me.

He turned up, read our twenty-year-old panel drawings without complaining, and handed back a system our own maintenance team could actually maintain. The documentation alone was worth the engagement.
